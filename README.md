# 🌍 Net Zero 2050 Analysis Dashbod
## 📌 Project Description
Net Zero 2050 is an Excel-based data analytics project focused on analyzing global energy transition trends, carbon emission reduction pathways, electricity generation transformation, and energy efficiency strategies required to achieve Net Zero emissions by 2050.

The project uses structured datasets, interactive Excel dashboards, charts, and analytical models to evaluate how renewable energy adoption, emission reduction policies, and energy demand optimization contribute toward sustainable development and climate goals.

The dashboard provides insights into CO₂ emissions, renewable energy growth, electricity generation mix, fossil fuel dependency, population impact, and energy demand trends across different years.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze global CO₂ emission reduction pathways toward Net Zero 2050.
- Study the transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy sources.
- Evaluate electricity generation transformation using sustainable energy sources.
- Monitor energy efficiency improvements and future energy demand trends.
- Compare renewable and non-renewable energy contributions over time.
- Visualize long-term sustainability and decarbonization strategies using interactive dashboards.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Sheets

### 🌱 CO₂ Pathways Analysis
Analyzes global carbon emission trends, population growth impact, per capita CO₂ emissions, and emission reduction pathways required to achieve Net Zero targets.

### ⚡ Energy Transition Analysis
Focuses on the transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy sources such as Solar, Wind, Hydro, and Nuclear energy while analyzing overall energy supply trends.

### 🔋 Electricity Generation Transformation
Provides insights into changes in electricity generation mix, including Renewables, Nuclear, Hydrogen-based systems, Coal, Natural Gas, Oil, and Fossil Fuels with CCUS technologies.

### ♻️ Energy Efficiency & Demand
Analyzes future energy demand patterns, energy efficiency improvements, and sustainable energy consumption trends.

## 📊 Key Insights Generated
- Identified the decline in global CO₂ emissions required to achieve Net Zero targets.
